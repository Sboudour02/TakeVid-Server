🔒 Privacy Notice

Welcome to TakeVid! Before you start, please understand how we handle your data:

What We Collect:

  • Cookies: Essential session cookies from YouTube/TikTok to access age-restricted or private videos
  • Video URLs: The links you paste are sent to our server for analysis
  • Download History: Stored locally on your device only
  • User Agent: Your browser information for compatibility

Why We Need This:

Cookies are necessary to bypass platform restrictions and download videos that require authentication. Without them, many videos won't be accessible.

⚠️ Important: Your cookies are sent to our secure server only for video extraction. We never store, share, or sell your data. Cookies are deleted immediately after processing.

Your Rights:

  • You can disable cookie sending in settings (some videos may not work)
  • You can clear your download history anytime
  • You can uninstall the extension to remove all local data
