# Privacy Policy for TakeVid

**Last Updated:** January 24, 2026

## Introduction

TakeVid is a browser extension that helps users download videos from YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, and Instagram. We take your privacy seriously and are committed to protecting your personal information.

## What Data We Collect

### 1. Essential Cookies
- **What:** Session cookies from YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, and Instagram
- **Why:** To access age-restricted, private, or geo-blocked videos that require authentication
- **How:** Collected from your browser only when you use the extension
- **Storage:** Sent temporarily to our server for video extraction, then immediately deleted

### 2. Video URLs
- **What:** The video links you paste into the extension
- **Why:** To analyze and extract download links
- **Storage:** Processed on our server and not stored permanently

### 3. Download History
- **What:** Titles, thumbnails, and quality of videos you've downloaded
- **Why:** To provide you with a convenient history feature
- **Storage:** Stored locally on your device only (not on our servers)

### 4. User Agent
- **What:** Your browser information (e.g., "Chrome 121 on Windows")
- **Why:** For compatibility with video platforms
- **Storage:** Sent to our server temporarily, not stored

## What We DON'T Collect

- ❌ Your personal identity or email
- ❌ Your browsing history outside of the extension
- ❌ Your passwords
- ❌ Any data when the extension is not actively in use
- ❌ Analytics or tracking data

## How We Use Your Data

1. **Video Extraction:** Cookies and URLs are sent to our secure server to bypass platform restrictions and extract download links
2. **Service Improvement:** No analytics - we don't track usage
3. **No Selling:** We never sell, rent, or share your data with third parties
4. **No Advertising:** We don't use your data for targeted advertising

## Data Security

- ✅ All communications use HTTPS encryption
- ✅ Cookies are deleted immediately after processing (within seconds)
- ✅ No permanent storage of sensitive data
- ✅ Server hosted on secure infrastructure (Render.com)

## Your Rights & Choices

You have the right to:

1. **Disable Cookie Sending:** Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Toggle "Send Cookies" off
   - Note: Some videos (age-restricted, private) may not work without cookies
2. **Clear History:** In the extension, go to History > Clear All
3. **Delete All Data:** Uninstall the extension to remove all local data
4. **Opt-Out:** You can choose to decline our privacy notice and not use the extension

## Third-Party Services

This extension interacts with:

- **YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram:** To fetch video content (subject to their respective privacy policies)
- **Our Backend Server:** Hosted on Render.com (US-based) for video processing

We do NOT use:
- Google Analytics
- Facebook Pixel
- Any tracking scripts

## Legal Compliance

- **CCPA (California):** We don't sell personal information
- **Children's Privacy:** This extension is not intended for users under 13
